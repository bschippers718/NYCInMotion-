Subway lines click to isolate · shift-click to add

Subway: MTA GTFS-RT, positions modelled along the track from the feed's ETAs; depth from MTA station structure data + OpenStreetMap tunnel/bridge tags. Buses: MTA Bus Time GPS snapped to their routes. Ferries: NYC Ferry GPS. Aircraft: ADS-B via adsb.fi / adsb.lol (altitudes above 1.5 km compressed to 40%). Taxi / Uber / Lyft have no live feed — particles replay the latest TLC month along real street routes (NYC street centerlines). Cameras: ten hand-picked NYSDOT highway cameras, live video via 511NY. Buildings & basemap: OpenFreeMap.
